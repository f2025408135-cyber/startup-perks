## 0. TL;DR — THE STRATEGY

**~48 programs are open to you.** The 2026 landscape is built on two gates:

1. **An incorporated company with a real website + domain-matched email.** This alone unlocks ~$8-18k in instant self-serve credits.
2. **A partner referral** (accelerator/VC/angel network). This is the master key: AWS Portfolio ($200k), Microsoft Investor Network ($100-150k), Google Scale ($200-350k), Datadog ($100k), GitHub ($10k), Vercel ($30k) — all locked behind codes. The big money is NOT available by cold-applying.

**Third gate (the biggest money of all):** equity funding from an *institutional* investor unlocks Claude for Startups credits, OpenAI VC-referral credits, Google Scale/AI tier ($350k), HubSpot 90%, Deel — and a small SAFE from one institutional investor unlocks ~$550-900k of face-value credits at once.

**The single biggest non-dilutive item is not a credit program — it's the Austrian aws Preseed Deep Tech grant (up to €267,000, no equity taken).** It requires an **Austrian company** and must be applied for **before or within 6 months of Firmenbuch registration**. If your partner is Austria-based, this drives the entire entity decision. See §1.

**Realistic year-1 capture:**
- Bootstrapped, no affiliation: **$20-40k real value** (median)
- With accelerator/angel affiliation (Org ID / referral): **$200-400k runway extension over 24 months**
- With Austrian deep-tech grant stack: **+€267k-1.15M in grants** on top

## 10. THE FIVE RULES THAT DECIDE ACCEPTANCE

1. **Identity coherence everywhere.** Legal name, domain, email domain, LinkedIn, billing accounts must match. AWS bot-scrapes your website; Google hard-rejects domain mismatches; Microsoft verifies legal entity + personal LinkedIn.
2. **Never apply before incorporation + live website.** All three hyperscalers reject unregistered entities.
3. **Report stage honestly.** "Bootstrapped" routes you to the right tiers (AWS Founders, Google Start, Cloudflare T3). Never dress a grant up as a round — Google excludes angel/F&F money from Scale.
4. **Write a 12-month spend projection with real services and units** (see ANSWERS_LIBRARY.md). Show full credit consumption before expiry — unspent credits block future top-ups.
5. **Never create a Datadog (or similar) account before the referral application** — "new customer" is a hard gate on the big programs.
